## What was built (the solution) — "After the Shahada" (ما بعد الشهادة), Track 03 of the AI in the Service of Islamic Content Challenge 2026
- The moment a person says the Shahada in the chat, a journey card appears in the same conversation and opens a full learning space.
- The curriculum: the book "Al-Wajeez: a classroom curriculum for the new Muslim" by Osoul Center — 4 units, 19 lessons — in the learner's language (10 editions converted: Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Indonesian, Portuguese, Russian, Bosnian, Vietnamese, Thai; answers explained in 20+ languages).
- First lesson: the meaning of the two testimonies; then the learner chooses: wudu, prayer, Al-Fatiha; the path adapts.
- Every answer sentence is tied to a page of the book; a code checker (not the AI) removes any sentence without a source; Qur'an verses and quotes are inserted from the sources (Tanzil, QuranEnc), never typed by the AI. The steps of wudu, ghusl and prayer are shown in the book's exact words.
- Listen-and-repeat recitation (Al-Minshawi's teaching recitation) for Al-Fatiha and short surahs; exercises from the book's own assessment questions; browse the whole book page by page.
- When the question is personal (marriage, job, family), a crisis, or danger to life: no AI ruling — a human mentor (a brother for brothers, a sister for sisters) from the Osoul team, and the emergency number of the person's country first if life is at risk.
- A follow-up platform for the mentors' team: cases inbox, crisis alerts, replies that reach the learner in the learning space, SLA timers.
- Sign in with Google or e-mail; daily lesson reminder by e-mail.
- Tested: 20 kinds of Shahada conversation (84/84 correct), ~470 end-user use cases, 284 + 450 new questions in 10 languages; 0 invented rulings; answers faithful to the book in 100% of the final checks; every self-harm message showed the emergency number first.
- Live at shahada.theislam.chat (demo).
