THE FIRST LESSON  |  «أوّلُ درس»
theislam.chat | After the Shahada (ما بعد الشهادة) — 2-minute film, v2 (after round-3 reviews of v1)
Runtime: first frame 00:00.0, last frame 01:59.5 (ceiling 120 s). 25 fps.
Timed at 2.1 words/s plus real breath gaps (0.3–0.5 s per sentence junction), not at 2.2.
=====================================================================================

LOGLINE
Someone types the two testimonies into a chat window. By the grace of Allah, that has happened in 166
conversations on theislam.chat. Then, too often, the conversation goes quiet. The film shows that moment twice:
the first time, the Shahada is the last message; the second time a card appears beneath it, and it becomes
the first lesson.

CONCEPT (the loop, unchanged from v1)
One frame, the Shahada typed in the chat and the congratulation under it, appears three times: as a blessing
(Beats 1–2, 6), as silence (Beats 7–8), and as an answer (Beat 14 the card slides in, in the same crop;
Beat 18 the bookend).
Owner's structure: PART 1 theislam.chat and what Allah has done through it (00:00–00:37.8) ·
PART 2 the problem (00:37.8–01:13.4) · PART 3 the solution (01:13.4–01:59.5).

BINDING RULES FOR THIS CUT
- NO REAL USER CONVERSATION TEXT appears anywhere. Every chat shot is the product demo (shahada.theislam.chat
  and the demo chat), recorded by the team, with a small fixed corner label on every such shot:
  «مشهد من العرض التجريبي | Product demo». Numbers come from the export; the PICTURES never do. Nothing is
  captioned "real message". No staged actions: no browser tab closing, no faces, no re-enacted "convert".
  Record Beats 1, 6, 7, 8, 14 and 18 on the same demo screen, same crop, same phone width, so the match-cuts hold.
- Wording: «الشهادتين / الشهادتان» for the act (the product name «ما بعد الشهادة» stays). Guidance is from Allah
  alone. We say "declared the Shahada in the chat", never "converted" or "166 converts". No pity, no deficit
  words: the gap is our trust. The full two testimonies are legible on screen at least once (Beat 1 freeze).
- "0 invented rulings" is always written "in testing".
- Qur'an: on-screen text only, Uthmani, copied from Tanzil character for character, with surah:ayah; never
  voiced by the synthetic voice; the listen-and-repeat shot is shown with its audio muted.
- Sound: narrator, room tone, keyboard taps, the product's message "pop", a hum of human voices (no lyrics),
  breath textures, silence. NO musical instruments.
- Numbers: only the brief's exact figures. No derived number in narration or on screen.
- Muted-first: one super per shot, at most 2 lines of ≤ 42 characters, inside the 4:5 safe area. Subtitles longer
  than 84 characters are split into two events.
- Pronunciation: theislam.chat is written «ذي إسلام دوت تشات». Check that ElevenLabs reads «بالشهادتين» as
  "bish-shahādatayn".

CONFLICTS AND HOW I DECIDED THEM
=====================================================================================
- The closing line. Problem (#10) and language want to keep «لا تكون آخرَ ما نسمعه منه»; amplify, creative and adab
  call it an overclaim. Lead ruling: «وبإذن الله، لا تكون الشهادتان آخرَ رسالة… بل أوّلَ درس.» It is literally true
  (the card always comes after the two testimonies), it ties the outcome to Allah, it uses the right term, and it
  matches the English super word for word.
- The opening line. Language asked to keep «هذه الرسالة كتبها إنسانٌ…», but the lead's ruling bans real user text,
  and a demo message was written by us. «رسالةٌ كهذه» keeps the line's voice and is true of every one of the 166.
- B4 number. Language and director prefer «أكثرُ من ستةَ عشرَ ألفَ». I kept the exact figure, because the owner's
  rule is "exact numbers only from the brief", and gave it 7.6 s with the grammar fixed («محادثةً»).
- B11 / B10 line. Language and director keep «فالصلاة لا تُعلَّم بالتخمين»; complete cuts it; problem replaces it.
  I took problem's «فبقي السؤال بلا درس»: the owner asked for the problem to be magnified, and this line states a cost
  instead of defending us. Same word count.
- B12. Creative, director, adab and problem all reject «أحدُهم أغلق الصفحة» (no data shows anyone closing a page;
  staged tab). Adab reframes the doubt about their prayer as our absence. I merged creative's plural with adab's
  wudu line: «والباقون لا نعرف أسماءهم، ولا من علّمهم الوضوء في اليوم التالي.» Language's «والأمانةُ أمانتُنا» against
  adab/problem's «علينا نحن»: I kept the meaning and the weight, and dropped only the filler «هنا».
- Order of the problem numbers. Creative wants a crescendo that ends on 11; nobody objected. Adopted: 89 → 22 →
  by design → 11 → "we don't know". The 11 now leads straight into the names we don't know, and into the
  "way back to them" super in Beat 14.
- Testimonial. Amplify wants [01:26–01:33]; four drafts and all the other reviewers keep [00:44–00:52], and the
  director lists it under "keep". I kept [00:44–00:52]: it reacts to the voice dialogue shown just before it, and
  "Subhanallah… Mashallah" carries warmth that a feature claim does not.
- Test proof. Amplify wants «صفرُ أحكامٍ مخترَعة» spoken; there was no time. Every reviewer agreed the v1 cards were
  unreadable. Lead ruling: one card held ≥ 3 s. It is now held for 3.5 s over the end of Beat 16, the beat about
  being faithful to the book, so it costs no extra time. "In testing" comes first on the card.
- Feature coverage against readability. Complete wants a new 3 s beat; director and hook want fewer layers. I
  resolved it by placing each feature where its word is spoken: sign-in and the daily e-mail under the card reveal
  (which also answers problem's blocker), listen-and-repeat and exercises after the word "Al-Fatiha", the book
  reader with the book. One super per shot.
