=====================================================================================
THE FIRST LESSON  |  «أوّلُ درس»
theislam.chat | After the Shahada (ما بعد الشهادة) — 2-minute film, v1 (showrunner merge of drafts 1–8)
Runtime: first frame 00:00.0, last frame 01:59.5 (hard ceiling 120 s, 0.5 s safety). 25 fps.
=====================================================================================

LOGLINE
One person types the Shahada into a chat window. By the grace of Allah, the same thing happened
in 166 conversations on theislam.chat. Then the conversation goes quiet. The film shows that moment
twice. The first time, the Shahada is the last message we ever hear from them. The second time,
a card appears under it, and it becomes their first lesson.

CONCEPT (the loop)
The opening frame (the typed Shahada and the congratulation bubble) comes back three times:
  1) as a blessing (00:00–00:08 and 00:31–00:38),
  2) as silence (00:38–01:12: empty chat, blinking cursor, dots going out),
  3) as an answer (01:15 the journey card slides in, in the SAME crop; 01:50 the bookend).
Owner's structure: PART 1 theislam.chat and what Allah has done through it (00:00–00:38) ·
PART 2 the problem, at full weight (00:38–01:12.5) · PART 3 the solution (01:12.5–01:59.5).

GLOBAL RULES FOR THIS CUT
- Voice: one male MSA documentary narrator (ElevenLabs), low, close-mic, unhurried. He narrates. He does not sell.
- Sound: narrator, room tone, real keyboard taps and the platform's own message "pop", and a hum of human voices
  (no lyrics). Breath textures and silence. NO musical instruments anywhere: no piano, strings, percussion,
  synth pads or risers.
- Qur'an: on-screen text only, Uthmani script copied from Tanzil (never retyped), with surah:ayah. The synthetic
  voice never recites Qur'an. The listen-and-repeat feature is SHOWN (screen, waveform, on-screen label) with its
  audio muted in the master. If Osoul's adab reviewer later approves real human recitation, it plays alone with
  every other track at -inf and nothing under it, and that alternate replaces nothing else.
- Adab: guidance is from Allah alone ("من فضل الله وحده"). We say "declared the Shahada in the chat", never
  "converted by us" or "166 converts". No pity and no deficit words: the gap is OUR trust (الأمانة), not their
  failure. No staged "convert" scenes: every chat is real footage, anonymised (names, e-mails, places blurred).
  No face of any new Muslim. Testimonial clip [01:02–01:13] is EXCLUDED.
- Numbers: only the exact figures in the brief. Narration speaks no derived number. On-screen sums appear only
  where they are plainly true ("11 of 166").
- Muted-first: every beat has one large on-screen line ("super") that tells the story with the sound off. Supers
  are English, centre-safe inside 4:5 so the same layer works for 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1. Arabic narration, burned
  English subtitles in the lower third (and an SRT for TV). Max 2 lines, max 42 characters per line.
- Pronunciation: the narration writes theislam.chat as «ذي إسلام دوت تشات».

WHAT I TOOK FROM EACH DRAFT, AND WHY
=====================================================================================
1 — Language: the core loop and both of its lines: the opening «هذه الرسالة كتبها إنسانٌ لم نرَه قط…» and the
    ending «لا تكون آخرَ ما نسمعه منه. تكون أوّلَ درس». Also «من فضل الله وحده», «ثمّ ماذا؟», the 11 line, «فالصلاة لا
    تُعلَّم بالتخمين», the closed-page passage, the rule list (open on a thing, no رحلة, no tricolons, no adjective
    on a number), and the phonetic domain. Why: it is the most human Arabic, and it ends on an image instead of
    a slogan.
2 — Amplify: the ranking of what to magnify (the 166 in silence, "every sentence has a page", the
    human-mentor boundary) and its truth guardrails. I left out its spoken "700+" (a derived number), the
    staged prayer-mat b-roll and the globe re-lighting to 166 (it implies outcomes).
3 — Complete: the coverage checklist. Every built feature is in the film, mostly as on-screen text and
    footage: live stages, Tanzil/QuranEnc, verbatim wudu/ghusl/prayer, exercises, the book reader, sign-in and
    reminder, mentor platform with SLA timers, all test figures, 20+ languages, Track 03, the demo URL.
4 — Creative: the "same screen three times" design (blessing, silence, answer), the title written letter by
    letter like the Shahada, and the first "answer" sound saved for the card. I dropped the staged "3 a.m."
    scene: no staging presented as real.
5 — Director: the timing discipline (every slot ≥ words ÷ 2.2, last frame 01:59.5), the three deliberate
    silences, the 166 dot field, the hard cut to silence before the title, the shot list (one continuous take
    for the card, same crop as the opening, no speed-ramps), the testimonial framing and the mix rules.
6 — Problem: the emotional shape of Part 2 (a slow fall, real chat footage, the dimming input field) and its
    rule of "no derived numbers". I left out its dramatised lonely man: it leans on pity and it is staged.
7 — Adab: the red lines that govern the whole cut: guidance from Allah alone, "declared the Shahada in the
    chat", no pity, «والأمانةُ هنا علينا نحن» (the gap is our trust), Qur'an only as on-screen Uthmani text from
    Tanzil (Al-Qasas 28:56), clip [01:02–01:13] excluded, no instruments, and the du'a «يا مقلّبَ القلوب…» moved
    to the end card as text so the film still closes on the loop line.
8 — Hook: the 3-second silent cold open with the typed Shahada and the super, muted-first supers on every beat,
    the dead-cursor hold as a pattern interrupt, "What do I do now?" growing to fill the frame, and the
    shareable super "Not the last message. The first lesson." The URL stays on screen only, because a spoken
    "try it now" would sell the moment.
