WHAT THIS CUT IS
A 3.5–4 minute film that follows the owner's outline: a question → theislam.chat and its numbers → a da'i's
testimony → the two testimonies, 166 times → "but where is the problem?" → the decision to build → how it was
built, step by step → it is all open → the close on shahada.theislam.chat. Built on v1 (the 2-minute merge) and
all eight reviews; the sentences the reviewers protected are kept.

RULES THIS SCRIPT FOLLOWS (from the brief, the owner and the 8 reviews)
- Voice: one male MSA documentary narrator (ElevenLabs), low, close-mic, unhurried. Warm, never triumphant, never
  selling. No AI clichés (no رحلة، ثورة، في عالمٍ يتسارع، نقلة نوعية، ليس مجرد، غيّر قواعد اللعبة), no stacked
  adjectives, no adjective on a number.
- Adab: guidance is from Allah alone («هذا من فضل الله وحده», «الهدايةُ من الله وحده»). The term is «الشهادتين».
  We say "declared the Shahada in the chat", never "converted by us" or "166 converts" (166 is a count of
  conversations). No pity, no deficit words: the gap is OUR trust («والأمانةُ هنا علينا نحن»). People-dots never
  fade. Brother for brothers, sister for sisters, said symmetrically.
- Real vs. demo: NO real visitor conversation text appears anywhere. Every chat on screen is either the platform's
  own UI with no visitor text, or the DEMO at shahada.theislam.chat, tagged «Product demo» in the corner. The 89 /
  22 / 11 beats are typography and abstract graphics, labelled as such. No staged scene is presented as real (no
  live typing of a "real" Shahada, no tab closing, no actors, no faces of new Muslims).
- Qur'an: on-screen text only, Uthmani, copied from Tanzil, with surah:ayah, in total silence. The synthetic voice
  never recites or quotes Qur'an. No recitation audio in the master; the listen-and-repeat feature is not used in
  this cut's sound.
- Sound: voice, room tone, Foley (keys, page turns, UI clicks), wordless human vocal beds (no lyrics), and designed
  non-tonal FX: air whooshes, filtered-noise risers, sub-impacts (designed thuds, not drums), reverse breaths,
  tape-stop drops, and deliberate silence. NO musical instruments: no piano, strings, percussion, synth pads or
  melodic risers.
- Numbers: only the exact figures from the brief and docs/evaluation.md. No derived number is spoken or shown
  (no "155", no "734", no "584"). "0 invented rulings" is always "in testing". 150,000 is NOT used (see FACT CHECK).
- Readable text: every super ≤ 2 lines of ≤ 42 characters; one super at a time; test cards held ≥ 2 s; the end
  card ≤ 3 lines; burned English subtitles in the lower third (SRT for TV); Arabic narration.
- Pronunciation (written phonetically in the narration): «ذي إسلام دوت تشات» = theislam.chat, «بي دي إف» = PDF,
  «جِت هَب» = GitHub, «جوجل» = Google.
