REVIEW OF v1 — DIRECTOR / EDITOR LENS (pacing, timecodes, footage feasibility, sound, <=120 s)
Reviewer: draft 5 (director)

VERDICT: MUST-FIX
SCORE: 7 / 10

WHY
The structure is right for TV: the same frame comes back three times, the silences are in the right
places, every number is exact, and the sound rules are clean. But the timecodes do not survive a real
voice read. The table claims "every slot >= words / 2.2", yet B3, B6, B12 and B19 have slots shorter
than their own words plus the pauses written inside them. B4, B8, B10, B11, B14, B15 and B16 have no
air at all. The runtime maths allows only ~2 s for breaths across ~25 line junctions; the real figure
is at least 0.3 s each, about 7 s. On an honest read the cut lands near 124 s. The last 10 s is also
unreadable: 3 test cards in 2 s and a 5-line end card in 2 s. About 6 s of narration has to go, and
that time should go to the tests and the end card.

  B12 9.0 s voice vs 20 w + pause = 9.6 s       OVER

EDITS (in order of importance)

1. beat 3: take the date out of the voice. Read «وصلت إلى ذي إسلام دوت تشات، وهو يحاور غيرَ المسلمين،
   كتابةً وصوتًا.» (12 w, 5.5 s). "since October 2024" is already in the lower third. Saves ~2.5 s.
   Re-time B3 to 00:08.5–00:14.5 and slide everything after it up 2 s.

2. beat 18 + beat 20: give the time saved to the close. Tests 3.0 s (1.0 s per card, hard cuts). Drop
   the footnote: nobody can read 4 facts in 0.7 s. It belongs in the YouTube description, not in the
   film. End card >= 3.5 s. Put the du'a on a card of its own for 1.5 s, or cut it. Five lines
   in 2 s is a broadcast QC fail.

3. beat 12: cut «لا نعرف اسمه،» (-3 w). Read «أحدُهم أغلق الصفحة بعد أن شهد. ولا نعرف إن صلّى في اليوم
   التالي. [pause] والأمانةُ هنا علينا نحن.» That fits 9 s with the pause. Also remove "the browser tab
   closes": it is a staged action presented as real. Dim the real crop to black instead.

4. beat 6: move the Al-Qasas 28:56 super out of the voice section and into the 1.5 s silent hold at
   00:37.0–00:38.5. Extend the hold to 2.0 s. Three supers while he speaks is too much text, and the ayah
   deserves the silence. Voice slot 00:31.0–00:37.0 then needs 6.4 s: trim the half-second pause to 0.3 s.

5. beat 16: "live and in real time (no speed-ramp)" is not feasible. The real ask panel takes several
   seconds per stage, and then a verse and the wudu panel must follow, all in 10.5 s. Keep "no speed-ramp",
   but allow straight JUMP CUTS between the three stages (each ~1.2 s, label legible). Cut «في الجواب»
   (-2 w) for air. Split the subtitle into two events: as written it is ~130 characters and breaks the
   42 x 2 rule.

6. beat 17: the first cut (listen-and-repeat for Al-Fatiha) does not match the narration, which is about
   personal questions and mentors. Move listen-and-repeat to the end of B15, after the "Al-Fatiha" choice,
   where the word is spoken. Drop the 0.5 s sign-in/reminder inserts; they can't be read at that length.
   B17 becomes three cuts of ~2.6 s each: question → handover, mentor inbox + crisis row + SLA timer,
   reply arriving in the learning space.

7. beat 15: too many shots and supers for 8.5 s (6 shots + a 1 s split + 3 supers). Cut «بلغته هو»
   (-2 w; "10 language editions" is on screen). Drop the 1 s EN|AR split screen. Show one super at a time:
   "Al-Wajeez · 4 units · 19 lessons" on the book, then "The path adapts" on the choice.

8. beat 4: the number words read slow in TTS («ستةَ عشرَ ألفًا وستُّمئةٍ وثلاثٌ وخمسون»). Either give B4
   7.5 s (from the B3 saving), or read «أكثرُ من ستةَ عشرَ ألفَ محادثة» while the graphic shows 16,653

9. beat 1 / beat 14 (feasibility): the export is text, so "a message types itself letter by letter" is a
   replay of a real message in the UI, not a captured live moment. That is acceptable, but change the super
