REVIEW OF v1 - LENS 1: ARABIC LANGUAGE, HUMAN VOICE, NO AI TELLS

VERDICT: APPROVE (with the edits below; none of them changes structure or timing)
SCORE: 8/10

What works: the narration reads like a person speaking. It opens on a thing (the message), the numbers carry no
adjectives, the silences do the emotional work, «ثمّ ماذا؟» and «فالصلاة لا تُعلَّم بالتخمين» land, «فلا فتوى من آلة»
echoes «لآلةٍ» in Beat 11 on purpose, and the ending closes the loop instead of using a slogan. None of the banned
vocabulary appears (رحلة، ثورة، في عالمٍ...), and the only list of three is the factual one.

What keeps it from 9-10: one pronoun clash you can hear (Beat 15), one spoken number that is a tongue-twister and
is missing a case ending (Beat 4), an abstract line where a short, concrete one would be stronger (Beat 12), and
small repetitions (له... له). The edits, in order of importance:

1. beat 15: replace «منهجُه كتاب «الوجيز» من مركز أصول، بلغته هو.» with «يقرأ فيه كتابَ «الوجيز» من مركز أصول، بلغته هو.»
   - why: in the original, the هـ in «منهجُه» refers to the place (مكانًا), the هـ in «بلغته» refers to the
     learner, and «يبدأ» follows with the learner as its subject. Heard aloud, that sounds like the place has a
     language. The fix gives the learner a verb («يقرأ»), so every pronoun in the beat points at one person.
     The word count does not change.

2. beat 4: replace «ستةَ عشرَ ألفًا وستُّمئةٍ وثلاثٌ وخمسون محادثة،» with «أكثرُ من ستةَ عشرَ ألفَ محادثة،»
   - why: seven words of case endings in one breath is where TTS stumbles and where a viewer stops listening.
     "More than sixteen thousand" is literally true, and 16,653 is already the big super, counting up on this
     exact word. This saves about 0.5 s.
     If the showrunner insists on the full figure, at least fix the grammar: «...وثلاثٌ وخمسون محادثةً»
     (the noun counted after a compound number takes the accusative: tanween fath). Otherwise ElevenLabs will
     read it in the wrong case or flatten it.

3. beat 12: replace «والأمانةُ هنا علينا نحن.» with «والأمانةُ أمانتُنا.»
   - why: «هنا» is filler, and «علينا نحن» is heavy for the last words before the black. Two words, with the
     key word repeated, hit harder and still say exactly what the adab draft wanted: the gap is our trust, not
     their failure. Subtitle: "And the trust is ours." The hard cut to silence lands better on a short line.
     This saves 2 words.

4. beat 14: replace «حين ينطق بها، تظهر له بطاقةٌ في المحادثة نفسها، تفتح له مكانًا يتعلّم فيه.» with
   «حين ينطق بها، تظهر بطاقةٌ في المحادثة نفسها، تفتح له مكانًا يتعلّم فيه.»
   - why: «له... له» twice in one sentence is a written tic you can hear. The first one adds nothing, because
     the card is visibly under his own message. This saves 1 word.

5. beat 3: replace «منذ أكتوبر ألفين وأربعة وعشرين، وهو يحاور غيرَ المسلمين، كتابةً وصوتًا.» with
   «موقعٌ يحاور غيرَ المسلمين منذ أكتوبر ألفين وأربعة وعشرين، كتابةً وصوتًا.»
   - why: «وهو» has no clear antecedent, because «ذي إسلام دوت تشات» is a foreign name with no grammatical
     gender, so the ear does not know what «هو» is. Naming it «موقعٌ» right after the name fixes that. It also
     puts the date after the verb, the order a speaker would naturally use. The word count does not change.

6. beat 15: replace «ثم يختار: الوضوء، أو الصلاة، أو الفاتحة.» with «ثم يختار ما يبدأ به: الوضوء، أو الصلاة، أو الفاتحة.»
   - why: a bare «يختار:» followed by a list sounds like a menu being read out. «ما يبدأ به» tells the ear what
     is being chosen and matches the super "The path adapts to their choice". If timing is tight, skip this
     edit; it adds 3 words (about 1.4 s), and edits 2-4 save more than that.

7. beat 8: replace «مِن هؤلاء، أحدَ عشرَ فقط» with «ومن هؤلاء جميعًا، أحدَ عشرَ فقط»
   - why: after 1.5 s of silence, a 1 s silence, and «ثمّ ماذا؟», a bare «هؤلاء» feels detached. «جميعًا» points
     back to the 166 and sets up the "only" contrast that the dot grid shows. It adds 1 word.

8. beat 16: no wording change, but set the voice direction: a real comma pause after «في الكتاب»,
   and say «يحذفه مدقّقٌ برمجيّ» slightly slower and drier.
   - why: this is the one technical sentence in the film. Without the pause, ElevenLabs runs it together and it
     sounds like a spec sheet. With the pause, it becomes a promise.

9. beat 5 (Arabic caption of the testimonial): replace «إنه مدهشٌ حقًّا. لقد أذهلني، في الحقيقة.» with
   «إنه مدهشٌ حقًّا، وقد أذهلني فعلًا.»
   - why: «في الحقيقة» set off by commas is a word-for-word copy of the English "actually", and it reads as
     machine translation. One line also reads more easily on screen.

10. beat 20 (end card du'a): keep it, but write it as «يا مقلّبَ القلوب، ثبّت قلوبَنا وقلوبَهم على دينك.» (with case
    endings on both)
   - why: it is the last Arabic the viewer reads. Partial tashkeel on «مقلّبَ» alone looks inconsistent; either
     vowel the whole phrase or none of it. Keeping it unlabelled is right, since it is adapted from the
     Prophetic du'a, not quoted.

TIMING EFFECT
Edits 2, 3 and 4 save about 6 words (about 2.5 s). Edits 6 and 7 add 4 words (about 1.8 s). Edits 1 and 5 change
nothing, and edits 8-10 are not spoken narration. Net: about 0.7 s shorter. Last frame 01:59.5 holds.
