REVIEW OF v1: CREATIVE LENS (draft 4)
Lens: is it unforgettable, is it gripping, is it TV-grade, and does it end on a line people repeat?

VERDICT: MUST-FIX (two truthfulness fixes in the loop shots; everything else is polish)
SCORE: 8/10 (9/10 once edits 1–3 are made)

WHAT ALREADY WORKS
- The "same screen three times" design survives, and the merge made it better. The opening frame stands
  for a blessing, then silence, then an answer, and the film is built around that one image.
- «ثمّ ماذا؟», the dead-cursor hold and «والأمانةُ هنا علينا نحن» are the strongest moments in all 8 drafts.
- "No page, no sentence." is a great super. It explains the product in four words.
- Keeping the first "answer" sound for the card is right.

EDITS (each keeps the total at or under 120 s)

1. beat 1 / 2 / 6: replace «Visual: … A message types itself letter by letter … (Real Shahada-moment footage, anonymised.)»
   with «Visual: the REAL Shahada text taken from the export (anonymised) and shown in the chat UI, no
   live-typing animation, small caption: "Real message from the platform export · re-rendered, anonymised"»
   Why: the brief's "Shahada moment" footage is a product recording. If a demo recording runs under
   «هذه الرسالة كتبها إنسانٌ لم نرَه قط», we are presenting a staged scene as real, and that breaks the
   film's own rule. Either the words come from the export and carry a caption, or the narration must
   change. Live typing would also be invented, because the export has sent messages, not keystrokes.
   This one decides the verdict.

2. beat 12: replace «أحدُهم أغلق الصفحة بعد أن شهد. لا نعرف اسمه، ولا نعرف إن صلّى في اليوم التالي.»
   (and the visual "the browser tab closes")
   with «لا نعرف أسماءهم. ولا نعرف إن صلّوا في اليوم التالي.» and use a plain slow fade to black, with no
   tab-close animation.
   Why: we have no data on anyone closing a page. "Ended within two messages" is not "closed the page
   after bearing witness", and the tab-close is a staged UI event. The new version is also shorter
   (−5 words, about −2.3 s), and the plural hits harder because it covers all of them, not one invented
   person. Use the saved time in edit 5.

3. beat 19 (the last line): replace «لا تكون آخرَ ما نسمعه منه. [stop] تكون أوّلَ درس.»
   with «لا تكون آخرَ رسالة. [stop] بل أوّلَ درس.»
   Why: the line people repeat must be short, even and literally true. «آخرَ ما نسمعه منه» promises we will
   keep hearing from them, which we cannot guarantee. «آخرَ رسالة» is a plain fact, because the card always
   comes after it. The new line also matches the English super word for word ("Not the last message. The
   first lesson."), so the voice and the screen say the same line in two languages and it becomes the one
   people share. «بل» gives the turn a sharper hinge than repeating «تكون». (−2 words.)

4. beat 8 ↔ beats 9–11 (order): move "ONLY 11" (beat 8, with its 166-dot grid) to come right after
   beat 11, just before the closing «لا نعرف أسماءهم…».
   Why: a fall should go from big to bad to worst. Right now the 11 lands first and the 89 and 22 dilute it.
   The new order is 89 ended → 22 asked → the machine could not teach → only 11 could be reached → we
   don't know their names. Each number then sets up the next line, and the dots that dim to 11 cut
   straight into the empty chat. Timing does not change.

5. beat 9: replace «00:49.5–00:51.0: NO VOICE» with «00:49.5–00:52.0: NO VOICE» (use 1 s of what edit 2
   saved)
   Why: the dead cursor is the film's pattern interrupt, and 1.5 s is too short to feel uncomfortable on
   TV. With 2.5 s the viewer starts to want someone to type, which is exactly the feeling the film is about.

6. beat 18: replace the three cards and the footnote in 2.0 s with «two cards, 1.0 s each: "84/84 Shahada
   conversations handled correctly" · "0 invented rulings"; the footnote moves to the end card in small type»
   Why: three cards plus a four-item footnote in two seconds cannot be read and looks like a ticker. Two
   readable numbers are worth more than five unread ones. "100% faithful" can go in the end card's small
   line.

7. beat 1 super: replace «Someone just declared the Shahada. / …in a chat window.»
   with «Someone declared the Shahada / …in a chat window.»
   Why: "just" suggests live footage. It is an archive message (see edit 1). Removing it costs nothing
   and keeps the hook honest.

8. beat 14: add a small corner caption «Product demo» over the continuous card take.
   Why: this shot reuses the beat-1 crop on purpose so it looks like the same chat. If beat 1 is real (edit 1)
   and beat 14 is a demo, the match cut must not suggest that a real person's chat got the card. One small
   label keeps the twist and the honesty, and viewers still feel the cut.

9. beat 13: replace «لهذا بنينا «ما بعدَ الشهادة».» with «لهذا… بنينا «ما بعدَ الشهادة».» (comma pause marked for TTS)
   Why: this line comes straight out of the one second of black, and a held breath after «لهذا» makes it
   land as a decision, not a transition. It costs about 0.3 s, which the edit-3 saving covers.
