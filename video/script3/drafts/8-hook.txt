SCRIPT 8 — "HOOK" LENS (attention and reach)
theislam.chat | After the Shahada — 2-minute film (118 s)
Specialist lens: stop the scroll in 3 s, a pattern interrupt every ~10 s, on-screen text that carries the story with the sound off, one shareable line, one clear call to action, TV master + vertical cut-downs.

======================================================================
FORMAT NOTES (apply to every beat)
======================================================================
- Master: 16:9, 1920x1080, 118 s. Vertical: 9:16, 1080x1920, same edit reframed (product footage is UI, so crop to the phone view / chat column; the Lawrence Brown clip is already vertical 480x720 and goes full-frame in 9:16, pillar-boxed with a blurred fill in 16:9).
- Muted-first: every beat has ONE large on-screen line (the "super") that tells the story alone. Supers sit in the centre-safe area (inside 4:5) so the same layer works in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5. English supers, Arabic narration, English burned-in subtitles in the lower third (separate SRT for TV).
- Numbers appear as big counters (Inter/Amiri, white on near-black or on the product's own background). A number is always on screen when it is spoken.
- Pattern interrupts (marked >>PI) roughly every 10 s: hard cut to black, a counter that falls, a silence, a sound drop-out, a speaker change (testimonial), a split screen, a UI "snap" to a new screen.
- Sound: soft ambient / nasheed WITHOUT instruments (voices/hum only), low under the voice. UI ticks and keyboard taps from real footage. No instruments at any point.
- Narration: Arabic MSA, warm, unhurried documentary voice (ElevenLabs). Pauses written as "…".
