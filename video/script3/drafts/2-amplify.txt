WHAT MUST BE MAGNIFIED — the 8 strongest claims, ranked
1. 166 conversations where a person declared the Shahada in the chat. The emotional peak, and it must land in near-silence, not with a counter animation.
2. Every sentence of an answer has a page in the book; a code checker (not the AI) removes anything without one; verses come from Tanzil/QuranEnc, never typed by the AI. This is the "scholar-grade" claim and the one that sets it apart from any chatbot.
3. Reach: 16,653 conversations, visitors from 150 countries, 52 languages (about 115,000 messages). Say it once, big and plainly, and always "by the grace of Allah".
4. The gap, said honestly: only 11 of 166 left a way to be contacted; 89 ended within two messages after the Shahada. Magnifying the problem is what makes the solution feel big.
5. Human mentors when it gets personal (a brother for brothers, a sister for sisters), and the country's emergency number first when a life is at risk. The AI knows where it should stop.
6. Tested: 0 invented rulings across 284 + 450 new questions in 10 languages; 84/84 Shahada scenarios; every self-harm message showed the emergency number first.
7. Seamless at the most important moment: the card appears in the same conversation, the instant the Shahada is said, and opens a full curriculum (Al-Wajeez: 4 units, 19 lessons, 10 language editions, answers explained in 20+ languages).
8. A respected outside voice: Dr. Laurence B. Brown calling it "an incredible tool". Proof that people outside the team see it the same way.
