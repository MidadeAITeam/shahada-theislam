SCRIPT 6 — "THE PROBLEM" LENS
theislam.chat | After the Shahada (ما بعد الشهادة) — 2-minute film, ≤ 120 s
Working title: «لا يُترك وحده» / "Not Left Alone"

Lens: the problem is the emotional core (≈ 0:32–1:19, ~47 s). The opening earns the joy,
the problem breaks it honestly, the solution lands as relief.
All numbers are exactly from the brief. The "imagine one of them" passage is clearly framed as
an illustration (تخيّل / قد يكون), never presented as a documented case; the on-screen label
"مشهد تمثيلي / Dramatization" appears over it.

Voice: one warm male documentary narrator (MSA), slow, unhurried; real silences are part of the script.
Sound: soft ambient pads / human-voice nasheed hum, no instruments. Keyboard taps and the chat
notification are the film's only "instruments".

  "ولا نعرف أين هم" (Beat 6) and "أو الفاتحة" → keep two choices only (Beat 16).

FACT CHECK (every number used)
  October 2024 · 16,653 conversations · 150 countries · 52 languages · 166 Shahadas · 11 reachable ·
  89 ended within two messages · 22 asked how to do wudu / pray / "what now" · 19 lessons · 4 units ·
  10 book editions. Nothing else is quantified. No figure is derived (e.g. no "155").
  Beat 11 states the design restraint honestly: the platform was deliberately not allowed to teach
  worship from a model's memory — the gap is presented as integrity, not as negligence.
  The man in Beats 8–13 and 19 is a labelled dramatization ("قد يكون" — may be), not a reported case.

THE 5 STRONGEST PROBLEM LINES (alternatives, drop-in replacements for Section 2)
====================================================================================================

1. قالها… ثم انطفأت الشاشة.
   He said it… and then the screen went dark.
   (Use in place of Beat 12, over the phone timing out.)

2. مئةٌ وستٌّ وستون شهادة… وأحدَ عشرَ بابًا فقط بقي مفتوحًا.
   166 Shahadas… and only eleven doors left open.
   (Alternative to Beat 6; pairs with the 166 → 11 lights.)

3. الشهادة سطرٌ واحد. أمّا الصلاة، فتحتاج من يعلّمها.
   The Shahada is a single line. Prayer needs someone to teach it.
   (Alternative to Beat 13, or a bridge into Beat 14.)

4. سأل: ماذا أفعل الآن؟ وكان أصدقُ جوابٍ عندنا: لا نعلّمك عبادتك من ذاكرة آلة.
   He asked: What do I do now? Our most honest answer was: we won't teach you your worship
   from a machine's memory.
   (Merges Beats 9 and 11 if time must be saved.)

5. دخل الإسلام في دقيقة… ثم وقف وحده أمام أول صلاة.
   He entered Islam in a minute… and then stood alone before his first prayer.
   (Alternative to Beat 13; keep the dramatization label on screen.)
