RED LINES FOR THE FINAL SCRIPT (10)
1. Guidance (الهداية) is attributed to Allah alone. Never "the AI guided 166 people", "our chatbot converted…", or "brought them to Islam". Use "من فضل الله"، "نطقوا بالشهادتين في المحادثة".
2. Do not claim to know hearts: say "نطقوا/أعلنوا الشهادتين في المحادثة", never "أسلم ١٦٦ شخصًا على أيدينا" or "ثبت إسلامهم". Never say "على يدنا/أيدينا".
3. Honour the new Muslims: no pity and no deficit words (ضائعون، تائهون، مساكين، "ignorant"). The gap is our responsibility ("الأمانة علينا"), not their failure. Do not use the testimonial clip [01:02–01:13] — its word "ignorant Muslims" will read badly in subtitles to a mass audience.
4. No real learner's name, face, country or chat text may be identifiable; blur all user messages and treat every conversation as an amanah. No staged "conversion" re-enactment presented as real.
5. Accurate religious wording: الشهادتان (not "the Islamic oath"), الوضوء، الصلاة، الغسل، الفاتحة. English: "the Shahada", "wudu", "prayer". Never call the Shahada "the end" or a "transaction completed".
6. No AI overclaims: the AI does not issue fatwas, does not teach from its memory, does not write verses. Keep "every sentence is tied to a page of the book" and "a code checker (not the AI) removes unsourced sentences". Never "the AI scholar/sheikh/imam" or "replaces the da'i/teacher".
7. Personal matters, crises and danger to life go to humans: a mentor (brother for brothers, sister for sisters) and the emergency number first. Never imply the AI counsels on marriage, family or self-harm.
8. Qur'an only as exact Uthmani text from Tanzil with surah:ayah citation, on screen; never recited by the synthetic (ElevenLabs) voice, never set to a beat or transition effect, never cropped mid-word. Only real human recitation (Al-Minshawi) may be heard. Any hadith shown must be verbatim with its source; adapted du'a must not be labelled as hadith.
9. No musical instruments anywhere (no piano, strings, drums, synth pads, "risers"); voice-only hum or silence. No exaggerated triumph tone, no "revolution", "first ever", "millions converted" or any number not in the brief; 16,653 may be spoken as "more than sixteen thousand" but never rounded up.
10. Osoul's institutional voice and humility: "we" means Osoul Association, credit the book Al-Wajeez by Osoul Center, close with a du'a rather than a sales slogan; no mocking of other faiths, no "before/after" contrast that belittles the person's past life or people.
